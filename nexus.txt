Adjust how long pings stay visible. As well as mute and disable certain pings.

[b]Options:[/b]

[b]Apply To[/b]
- Use Settings on Your Pings
- Use Settings on Teammates' Pings

[b]Duration (seconds)[/b]
- Enemies Duration
- Companion Targets Duration
- Materials & Items Duration
- Health Stations Duration
- Location Pings Duration
- Threat Warnings Duration
- Attention Warnings Duration

[b]Visibility[/b]
- Disable Enemies
- Disable Companion Targets
- Disable Materials & Items
- Disable Health Stations
- Disable Location Pings
- Disable Threat Warnings
- Disable Attention Warnings
